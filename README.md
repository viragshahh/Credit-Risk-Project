## Project Overview
This repository contains the interactive research analytics platform and capstone presentation website for the comparative Financial Risk Analytics study of:
* **Primary Case**: ICICI Bank (Private Bank) vs. Bajaj Finance (Upper-Layer NBFC)
* **Banking Peer Group**: HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank
* **NBFC Peer Group**: Shriram Finance, Cholamandalam Finance, Mahindra Finance
* **Research Horizon**: FY2015 – FY2025 (11 Financial Years)

## Key Modules Included
1. **Executive Dashboard & Head-to-Head Benchmarks**
2. **11-Year Financial & Credit Risk Time Series (FY15–FY25)**
3. **Credit Risk Lab**:
   - Days Past Due (DPD) Delinquency Analysis (0 to 90+ DPD)
   - Interactive Logistic Regression PD Scorecard (ROC-AUC 0.8597, Gini 71.95%)
   - 36-Month LGD Vintage Recovery Curve (Terminal Recovery 32.82%, LGD 67.18%)
   - Ind AS 109 / IFRS 9 Multi-Scenario ECL Staging Model
4. **Vasicek IRB Capital Engine**:
   - Basel III Asymptotic Single Risk Factor (ASRF) formula simulator vs Standardized Approach
5. **Market Risk, Value at Risk & Kupiec Backtesting**:
   - 99% 1-Day & 10-Day VaR (Parametric, Historical, Monte Carlo) and Expected Shortfall
   - Kupiec POF Backtesting (Basel Green Zone verification)
6. **Financial Shocks Event Study**:
   - 15 historical stress events with OLS Market Model CAR[-1,+1], CAR[-5,+5], CAR[-5,+10]
7. **Empirical Academic Hypothesis Testing**:
   - 5 formal econometric hypotheses with statistical proofs
8. **Oral Defense Presentation Deck**:
   - Dedicated slide-by-slide presentation mode for classroom defense
9. **Deliverables Repository**:
   - Master Excel Analytics Workbook (26 sheets)
   - 28 website-ready CSV datasets
   - Comprehensive Academic Research Report (75+ pages equivalent)
